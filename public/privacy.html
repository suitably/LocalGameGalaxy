Privacy Notice

for games.nexumia.de and the Nexumia Android app · Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no cookies, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.
  • Scores, names and settings stay on your device.
  • Data only goes to the services listed below when you play with several devices, turn on notifications or load certain content. Each section says what, when and why.
  • We do not store any data about you on a server.

1. Controller

Nexumia
E-mail: support@nexumia.de

We have no data protection officer and are not required to have one. Write to the e-mail address above with any question about your data.

2. Visiting the website (hosting)

The website is hosted and delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, Cloudflare processes the connection data it needs to deliver the site and protect it from attacks: IP address, date and time, the address requested, amount of data, browser and operating system (user agent) and the referring page.

We do not analyse this data and have not turned on access logs. Cloudflare processes it as our processor (Art. 28 GDPR) and deletes it under its own retention rules. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR).

3. Data on your device

The games store in your browser (local storage, IndexedDB, service worker cache) or in the app: settings, player names, scores, room codes, downloaded songs and scores, and the app's files for offline use. This data does not leave your device except in the cases below. We set no cookies.

Storing it is strictly necessary for the features you use (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German implementation of the ePrivacy Directive). You can delete everything at any time: in your browser's settings ("clear site data") or on Android ("clear app storage").

When you sing in Melodiq, the app asks for your microphone. The sound is analysed on your device only; the recording itself is neither sent anywhere nor stored.

4. Playing with several devices

So that phones and the TV can play together, the games use public services. Legal basis in each case: our legitimate interest in providing the multi-device feature that you start yourself (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). When you play on one device only, none of these services is used.

4.1 Game state over MQTT

GuessArt, Storyteller, Tabletop, the party games and room games exchange the game state through public MQTT servers: first broker.hivemq.com (HiveMQ GmbH, Germany), and if it is down broker.emqx.io (EMQ Technologies Co., Ltd.; servers may be outside the EU).

  • What: your IP address, the game state with everything you enter in the game (player names, texts, drawings, moves) and a random device id.
  • Important: these servers are public. Anyone who knows the room code can read the game state. The latest state stays on the server until it is overwritten. Do not enter real names or confidential content.
  • Using broker.emqx.io may transfer data to a country without an adequacy decision, where protection equal to the EU's is not guaranteed. This happens only when HiveMQ cannot be reached.

4.2 Direct connection over WebRTC (Melodiq)

Melodiq connects phone and TV directly (WebRTC). To find each other, the devices announce themselves to public WebTorrent trackers (tracker.openwebtorrent.com, tracker.webtorrent.dev, run by volunteers of the WebTorrent community) and ask STUN servers for their public address (stun.cloudflare.com of Cloudflare, Inc., USA; global.stun.twilio.com of Twilio Inc., USA; both certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). These services receive your IP address and a random party id. Connected devices see each other's IP addresses. In the connection settings you can add your own trackers and STUN servers and turn off the default ones.

5. Notifications

When you allow notifications (consent through your browser's prompt, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG), the games tell you when it is your turn. You can withdraw consent at any time in your browser or app settings.

  • Web Push: your browser creates a push address at its maker's push service (e.g. Google, Mozilla, Apple). Another device in your game sends an encrypted message ("your turn") to that address through our Cloudflare Worker. The worker stores nothing; the push service receives the push address, the encrypted message and your device's IP address. Google LLC, Mozilla Corporation and Apple Inc. are based in the USA.
  • ntfy: only if you choose ntfy, your ntfy topic and the notification text go unencrypted to ntfy.sh (public service of the ntfy project; servers may be outside the EU) or to the ntfy server you enter.

7. Services you set up yourself

Optional features connect your device directly to services you enter yourself: your own companion server, signaling server, ntfy or STUN servers, a USDB account or a GitHub token for feedback. You decide what is sent; their own privacy policies apply. Feedback you send through GitHub is stored there as a publicly visible issue.

8. Android app

The app contains the same games and processes data the same way as the website. It contains no Google services and no ad or analytics libraries, and asks only for the permissions a feature needs (e.g. the microphone for Melodiq).

9. Your rights

You have the right to

  • access your data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18),
  • data portability (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21),
  • withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)),
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your residence or place of work (Art. 77).

As we store no data about you on servers, we usually cannot report on data that exists only on your device. You can view and delete it yourself.

10. Other information

You do not have to provide any data to use the games; without the services above, only the multi-device features, notifications and third-party content do not work. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling. When the games change, we update this notice; the version published here applies.
