- **Changelog fragments and an automated release PR** (#593): PRs add `changelog.d/<slug>.<type>.md` and `docs/log.d/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` instead of editing `CHANGELOG.md` and `docs/log.md`, so parallel PRs no longer conflict there. `changelog-release.yml` collects them weekly (or on demand, with a bump or exact version) into a `release/vX.Y.Z` PR that bumps every `package.json`, lockfile and the Android `versionName`/`versionCode`; merging it tags the release, and the release text is the changelog section. `scripts/changelog.mjs` (`check`, `next-version`, `release`, `notes`, `verify`, …) does the work; `check:docs` accepts a fragment and the `no-changelog` label.
