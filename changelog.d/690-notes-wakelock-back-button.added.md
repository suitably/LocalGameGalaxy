- **Melodiq Notes keeps the screen on and respects the Android back button** (#690): the stage holds the shared screen wake lock while a song plays and releases it on pause or leave. The back button closes an open immersive view first, then pauses and leaves a running stage; dialogs still close first, and an idle stage uses the app default. Screens can take part through the new `registerBackHandler` in `src/lib/backButton.ts`.
