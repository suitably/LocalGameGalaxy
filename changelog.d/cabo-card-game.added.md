- **Cabo card game**: new card game in the Kartenspiele category for 2–6 players on one device. Four face-down cards each, peek at your bottom two at the start of a round, swap or discard drawn cards, special powers (7/8 peek own, 9/10 peek opponent, J/Q blind swap), calling Cabo for a last round, exactly 100 falls back to 50. The device is handed over between turns so hidden hands stay private; an optional memory aid keeps seen cards face up.
