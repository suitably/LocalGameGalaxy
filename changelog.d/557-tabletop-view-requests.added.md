- **Tabletop: ask to see another player's cards** (#557): the eye button asks another player to show cards from their hand, picked by position or "any N"; the request can be changed or withdrawn while it waits. The owner gets a notice with the asked cards marked and shows them, a random pick or a chosen subset, or says no. Shown cards reach only the asking player (sealed for that phone) until they close the view; in pass and play they appear behind the handover cover.
