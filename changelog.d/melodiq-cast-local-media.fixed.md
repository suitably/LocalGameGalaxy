- **Melodiq: songs from a local folder play on a cast TV**: instead of "No Audio Source Found", the song's audio and video are now sent to the TV first. Songs from a companion server also play on a cast TV, which has no server settings of its own. A TV that connects while a song is running now joins it at the current position instead of staying at the start, and the host goes back to the song list because the song now plays on the TV. Picking a song right after connecting a TV no longer opens the session on the host for a moment. The song progress bar no longer repaints over the video on every frame.
