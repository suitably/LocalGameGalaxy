- **Melodiq Notes keeps recordings in time** — a recording without bar marks, or with marks from an older alignment, is aligned as soon as it is on stage and stored with its score, so it no longer plays at the score tempo with only a start point. Scores with drums whose chroma match fails (long empty intro) fall back to the drum attacks, and a last bar is no longer stretched into a fade-out. The recording panel checks the result (suspect bars, implausible tempo) and offers steady tempo, bar by bar or score tempo; marks corrected by hand are never replaced.
