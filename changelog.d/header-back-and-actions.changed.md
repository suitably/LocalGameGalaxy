- **One top bar in Cards, Imposter, Sudoku, Wordle and Melodiq**: the back arrow in the global header is the only way back, and game actions moved from icon rows in the page into the header (on phones the less used ones sit in the header menu). Cards: back to the game selection, undo, round history and restart are header actions, and restarting asks before it deletes all rounds; the -1 / +1 buttons are larger and labelled for screen readers. Imposter: the info button sits in the header and the result screen has only "Play again". Sudoku: statistics and rules are header actions, and switching the difficulty during a started game asks before it discards the board. Wordle: statistics, history, duel and rules are header actions (the extra statistics button after the daily puzzle is gone), and the rules dialog closes with "Close". Melodiq: the header back arrow leads from a playlist back to the playlist list, and the phone connection screen shows its name in the header instead of an own back button and heading; the song-folder prompt is translated and follows the theme.
- **Werewolf role editor**: the full-screen editor has a close button in its title bar, edit and reset/delete buttons have tooltips and screen-reader labels, default roles show a reset icon, dialogs cancel with "Cancel" and night abilities are skipped with "Skip".
