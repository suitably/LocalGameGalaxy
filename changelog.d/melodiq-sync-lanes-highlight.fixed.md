- **Melodiq stays in sync after a pause, seek or reload**: the vocal track no longer keeps a small offset to the instrumental or takes many seconds to catch up (now back within a few milliseconds in about a second), and it waits while the instrumental buffers. A TV follows the host's position within a few hundredths of a second instead of half a second, so lyrics, pitch lanes and scoring match what is heard; phone lyrics follow the host more closely as well.
- **Melodiq on a phone shows your own pitch lane**: a phone that hosts without a TV shows only the singers on its own microphone, and a joined phone shows its own singer; with a TV connected the hosting phone keeps all lanes.
- **Melodiq lyrics highlight is easier to see**: the sung part of the line is now filled in the singer's colour and the current syllable glows, instead of a single coloured syllable between white ones.
