- **Melodiq Notes: import several scores with recordings** — "Import several scores" under the Notes library takes many score files at once (`.gp`, `.mxl`, `.musicxml`, `.xml`, `.mid`). Each goes to its song folder (matched by artist and title, from the score or an "Artist - Title" file name). A folder without a recording gets the top YouTube hit, and every score is aligned to its recording automatically. In "Manage files", "Search for another recording" swaps a wrong mix for another video and aligns the folder's scores again.
