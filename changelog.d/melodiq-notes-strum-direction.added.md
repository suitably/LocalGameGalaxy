- **Melodiq Notes shows the strum direction on guitar chords**: an arrow above each stroke, downstrokes on the beats and upstrokes on the offbeats (sixteenth strumming included). Direction marks in MusicXML (up-bow / down-bow) and Guitar Pro (pick stroke, brush) win over the rhythm. In "Parts", each guitar part can be set to all down or all up, for the whole song or per bar; the setting is stored with the score in its song folder.
