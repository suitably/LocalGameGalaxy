- **Melodiq Notes: notes seconds off after a section the score and the recording do not share**: when the recording has a part the sheet music lacks (an extra chorus, a solo, a repeat that is not written out), or the score has one the band did not play, every later bar ended up off by that section's length (seconds, consistently). The alignment can now leave such a section out in the middle, not only at the start or end: the notes wait through a section only the recording has and skip one only the score has. Realign a song to get the fix.
