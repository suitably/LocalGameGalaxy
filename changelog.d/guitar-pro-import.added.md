- **Guitar Pro import in Melodiq Notes:** Guitar Pro 7/8 files (`.gp`) open like MusicXML and MIDI. Every track becomes a part (guitars, bass, drums), with repeats, ties, tuplets and tempo changes; drum notes keep the kit's sounds (hi-hat open/closed, rimshot, ride bell, …). Song folders on the companion server accept `.gp` scores too. Older formats (`.gp5`, `.gpx`) are not supported.
