- **Spieltisch routines can deal, take turns and react**: button routines gain `DEAL` (cards to every hand), `TAKE_FROM_BAG`, `PLACE_IN_HOLDER`, `SET_STATE`, `TURN` (next/previous/caller, writes the seat's turn), per-seat `ROLL` and `COUNTER` ("roll all my dice"), and `IF` with one comparison. Buttons can run by themselves on `onDrop`, `onTurnStart`, `onEnterZone` and `onCounter`. The host now decides every random result (dice, shuffle order, spinner, bag pieces) from a seed it stamps on the action, and imported VirtualTabletop / playingcards.io buttons keep simple `clickRoutine`s (shuffle, move cards, roll, flip, timer).
