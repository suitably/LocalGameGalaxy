- **Melodiq: phone pitch after a reconnect**: a phone that reconnected during a song (reload, network change, app in the background) was shown again but scored nothing, because the host still read the pitch of its old connection.
- **Melodiq: phone role and microphone no longer mix**: a phone's permission role (admin, queue manager, …) and "sings in this song" shared one value. A roster update — for example after someone changed their profile — could switch an admin's microphone off mid-song, and a song start or end took the admin rights away on the phone until the next roster update (also after reconnecting). The phone now follows the host's player list for the microphone and keeps its role.
- **Melodiq: phone keeps singing in the background**: the pitch is sent from a timer that browsers do not pause in a background tab, the screen stays on while the phone sings, and a phone that returns to the foreground or comes back online searches for the host at once.
- **Phone connection**: a slow connection attempt (mobile data, TURN relay) gets 10 s instead of 6 s, and overlapping connection attempts no longer open duplicate connections.
