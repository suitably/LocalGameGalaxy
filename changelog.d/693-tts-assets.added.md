- **Imported Tabletop Simulator games show their images, also offline**: image links are switched to `https://` for every host (Elfenland's `http://i.imgur.com` images were blank), and the import saves a copy of every image on this device with a progress bar. Images that cannot be loaded directly come through the app's own proxy (Steam and imgur only) or the companion server; unreachable image hosts are listed in the import report instead of being retried forever. 3D models (`Custom_Model`) show their texture as a sprite, or a labelled token, instead of being left out.
