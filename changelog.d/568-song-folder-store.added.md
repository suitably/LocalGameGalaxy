- **One song folder for Melodiq and Melodiq Notes** (#568): a song's karaoke `.txt`, its mix, any number of scores (MusicXML/MIDI) with their bar marks and the recordings made in Melodiq Notes now live together in one song folder on the companion server, and the song is one entry in the Notes library, where all its scores play with the same mix. A folder with only sheet music is a song too (listed in Notes, not in Melodiq's karaoke list); its `song.json` keeps a stable id, title, artist and score labels. Saving from Notes writes into the song folder, or creates one for a new song. On first start the server copies the old Notes library (`NOTES_DIR`) into song folders and keeps the originals until the migration is confirmed. The old `/api/notes/pieces…` routes and the song-score routes are replaced by song-folder routes.
