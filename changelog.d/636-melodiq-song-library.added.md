- **Melodiq song library** (#636): the song list gets tabs for All, Artists, Albums, Favourites and Playlists. Artists and albums open like an accordion, every song has a heart to mark it as a favourite (also in the song menu), and the search now also finds albums and matches several words. Favourites and playlists are saved per API user on the companion server; a playlist can be shared, then other users can see and play it but only its owner can change it. With a local song folder they are saved on the device. The album comes from the UltraStar `#ALBUM` tag or `song.json`. Existing server playlists are taken over once (shared, without the stored token); the old `/api/playlists` routes and the playlist sync switch are gone.
