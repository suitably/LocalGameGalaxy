- **Golf, Mau-Mau and Schwarzer Peter**: three classic card games for one device, with the rules enforced. In **Golf** (2–6 players) each player has six cards in a grid; matching columns score zero, and a game is nine holes. **Mau-Mau** (2–5 players) uses the 32-card deck: 7 makes the next player draw two (stackable), 8 skips, and a jack wishes a suit. In **Schwarzer Peter** (Old Maid, 2–6 players) players draw from each other's hands and pair cards up, and whoever is left holding the black jack loses. Hidden hands are handed over behind a cover between turns.
