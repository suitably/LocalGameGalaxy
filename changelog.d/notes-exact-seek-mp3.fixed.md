- **Melodiq Notes: notes seconds off after a seek or loop in older songs**: browsers seek in MP3 recordings only by an estimate that can land seconds off, so after a jump, a loop restart or "Practise bar N" the notes ran consistently early or late. Recordings the browser cannot seek in exactly (MP3, Ogg, local files of those types) are now decoded once and played from the decoded samples, the same ones the alignment measured; M4A and WAV play as they are. The fine-tune slider now reaches ±10 s.
