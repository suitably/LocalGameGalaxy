- **One top bar in GuessArt, Storyteller, Gartic Phone and Knister (party room)**: running games no longer hide the app header or draw a second bar. The header back arrow leaves the game (to the game setup, or to the party lobby in room mode), the header shows the game's title (GuessArt: players, round and the secret word; Storyteller: story, turn and writer), and the game actions (switch local/remote, share links, edit, history, read story, invite players, share lobby, end game for everyone) sit in the header — on phones the less frequent ones are in the header menu. The in-body "Zur Lobby"/"Zur Party" buttons and Gartic's extra "Menü" button are gone; Gartic's round and phase chips now stay visible on phones, and ending a Gartic game uses the standard confirmation dialog.
