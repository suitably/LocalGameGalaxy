- **Melodiq Notes: guided practice of weak bars** (#638): "Practise bar N" in the results now starts a guided practice. The bar (with the next one) loops at 75 %; three passes in a row at 90 % or more raise it to 100 %, and three more there mark it as done. A congratulation then offers the next weakest bar of the performance, and once every weak bar is done, the whole song at 50 % or a new performance. A chip on the stage shows bar, speed and passes; it can be stopped at any time. The speed popover gets one-tap buttons for 50 / 75 / 100 % and steps of ±5 / ±10 %.
